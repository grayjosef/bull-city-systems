# Bull City Systems brand marks
Adopted October 20, 2026 by Joey Gray, after a two-round board review (Muse + ChatGPT concepts) and a Durham culture check.

## The system
- **Primary mark (A1, "Swiss Geometry")**: one line draws the B, bows into the bull's head, exits as the gold S.
  - `logo-mark-cream.png` - cream line on transparent, for dark surfaces (site nav, footer). The site's BCSMark component serves this.
  - `logo-mark-navy.png` - original navy line, for light surfaces and documents.
- **Seal (A6, "Negative Space Seal")**: the same mark reversed inside a navy disc. Avatars, stamps, favicons, social profiles.
  - `seal.png`, `seal-512.png`; site favicon.ico, favicon-64.png, and apple-touch-icon.png are generated from it.
- **Ceremonial bull (B3, "Calligraphic Motion")**: the full broadside bull, Durham's native form since the Bull Durham tobacco era.
  - `bull-b3-cream.png` (dark surfaces; used in the site's About section), `bull-b3-navy.png` (light surfaces).
  - Reserved for signage, sponsorship banners, community presence.
- **OG image**: `../og-image.png` (1200x630) composes the cream primary mark on ink.

## Colors
- Ink #0B0D11, Bone #ECE5D6, Brass #C8932E (site tokens --c-ink, --c-bone, --c-brass); mark navy sampled from the source art.

## Rules
- Never redraw the mark with a mouth, eyes, or facial detail; the complaint that killed round 1.
- Documents and headers: monogram (A1). In Durham, in person: the whole bull (B3).
- Source renders (ChatGPT, Oct 20) live in the conversation uploads; a clean vector rebuild remains the open production step for print-grade use.
